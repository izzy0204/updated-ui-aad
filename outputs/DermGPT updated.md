- **Inquiries or to report an issue:** No clear contact / FAQ located in the reviewed sources. [4]
- **Release Stage:** Not specified.

- **Regulatory Approval Status, if applicable:** Not FDA approved / not described as a regulated medical device. [1,4]
- **Global Availability:** Not specified.

- **Keywords:** Not specified.

## 2) Uses & Directions
- **Intended Use & Workflow:** Intended as an assistant for dermatologists and staff to support practice management tasks and access dermatology knowledge; not a stand-alone decision maker or replacement for medical care/decision-making. [1]
- **Primary Intended Users:** Dermatologists and dermatology staff (RNs, MAs, ancillary staff); some sources describe broader audiences, but primary positioning is clinical workflow support. [1,4]
- **Necessary knowledge/expertise:** No special technical expertise described; clinical expertise required to appropriately apply outputs. [1,2]
- **How to Use:** Use via the web app by prompting the chatbot; outputs are described as including citations and requiring proofreading. [2,4]
- **Targeted patient population:** Not specified; tool is primarily provider-facing. [1]
- **Patient consent or disclosure required or suggested?:** Not specified.

## 3) Warnings
- **Developer-Supplied Warnings:** AI can create inaccuracies; users should proofread content. [2]
- **Model Limitations:** Not a substitute for professional medical advice; limitations include dependence on curated literature and typical LLM risks (incorrect outputs/hallucinations), mitigated by clinician oversight. [1,2]
- **Skin type representation & equity:** Not specified.
- **Clinical Risk Level:** Not explicitly stated; likely low-to-moderate because it is a language assistant that could still produce incorrect medical content if misused. [6]

## 4) Trust Ingredients

- **Interaction with other systems:** Not specified.
- **Outcomes & Output:** Natural-language answers and drafts for clinical/admin tasks (e.g., patient education, prior authorizations, messaging responses, study support). [1,2,4]
