## 5) Key Metrics
### Usefulness / Efficacy
- **Goal of Metric(s):** The primary goal of DermGPT is to help healthcare providers with clinical tasks to increase productivity and save time. [1,9,10] Specific goals include improving office productivity by reducing the time physicians spend on electronic health records (EHR), de-escalating moderate to difficult clinical tasks to easy ones, and improving answer clarity and conciseness in dermatology-related queries. [1,9,10]
- **Result:** In Dr. [1,9,10] Kamangar’s practice, DermGPT has enabled them to save at least 30 to 60 minutes at the end of the day typically spent updating the electronic health record, allowing them to complete the clinic day much earlier. [1,9,10] In a comparative study, DermGPT’s answers were generally preferred over ChatGPT’s (48.1% vs 28.4%; χ2 test P=.04). [1,9,10] Specifically, attending physicians preferred DermGPT's answers in 47.7% of cases (vs 28.7% for ChatGPT), and residents preferred DermGPT's answers in 48.9% of cases (vs 27.8% for ChatGPT). [1,9,10] However, ChatGPT's references were preferred overall (46% vs 23.5% for DermGPT). [1,9,10]
- **Interpretation:** DermGPT's ability to save 30-60 minutes daily in clinical practice indicates a significant improvement in office productivity for dermatologists. [1,9,10] The study results suggest that users generally find DermGPT's answers to be clearer and more concise, making it accessible for quick clinical reference. [1,9,10] This indicates strong potential for improving answer quality in dermatology-related queries. [1,9,10] However, the study also highlights that ChatGPT's more robust source citations enhance trust, suggesting a potential area for improvement for DermGPT in terms of perceived academic rigor and credibility of sourcing. [1,9,10]
- **Test Type:** The usefulness and efficacy of DermGPT were evaluated through real-world application in Dr. [1,9,10] Kamangar's practice and a double-blind comparative study. [1,9,10] The study involved a survey where dermatologists (attending physicians and residents/fellows) rated and preferred answers generated by DermGPT and ChatGPT for a list of dermatology-related questions. [1,9,10] Statistical analysis was conducted using χ2 tests to assess the significance of preferences. [1,9,10]
- **Testing Data Description:** A list of dermatology-related questions authored by two dermatology residents (CZ and NMG) was posed to both DermGPT and ChatGPT. [1] Three questions to which DermGPT did not provide a response were excluded as non-evaluable item pairs. [1] The answers from both models for each question were paired and assigned as A or B using a computer-generated randomization list. [1] Identifiable metadata and formatting were cleared from the responses. [1]
- **Validation Process and Justification:** A double-blind comparative study was conducted to evaluate DermGPT's performance against ChatGPT. [1] A survey was distributed via email and QR codes to dermatologists (faculty, residents, and fellows) at the University of California, Irvine, and the University of California, Davis. [1] Survey takers were informed that both responses were produced by LLMs but were blinded to which model produced which response. [1] They were asked to choose their preferred answers based on quality, specifically which answer they thought would be best suited for patient care or was most accurate. [1] The rating options included 'Model A better', 'Model B better', 'Equal quality', and 'Both inadequate'. [1] Statistical analysis was performed using SAS OnDemand for Academics (version 9.4), employing χ2 tests to assess significance (P<.05). [1] Interrater reliability was not prespecified or assessed; ratings were aggregated at the item level. [1] The study used a voluntary, anonymous survey and did not require institutional review board review as no identifiable information was collected and it posed minimal risk. [1]
- **AUROC/Accuracy/etc.:** In a comparative study against ChatGPT, DermGPT's answers were preferred overall by 48.1% of respondents compared to ChatGPT's 28.4%, with the χ2 test showing significance (P=.04). [1,9,10] Specifically, in the attending physician group (n=195 responses), DermGPT's answers were preferred 47.7% of the time (93 responses) compared to ChatGPT's 28.7% (56 responses). [1,9,10] In the resident group (n=90 responses), DermGPT was preferred 48.9% of the time (44 responses) versus ChatGPT's 27.8% (25 responses). [1,9,10] In Dr. [1,9,10] Kamangar's practice, DermGPT has been able to save at least 30 to 60 minutes at the end of the day typically spent updating electronic health records. [1,9,10]
