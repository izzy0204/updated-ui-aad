## 2) Uses & Directions
- **Intended Use & Workflow:** Tandem Browser is intended for users who want to integrate AI agents directly into their daily browsing experience, enabling them to automate web tasks, rewrite live UI, and gather information from websites in a shared human-AI workspace. [3,4] The workflow involves the AI agent operating within the user's browser, seeing the same DOM, network requests, and accessibility tree. [3,4] The agent can perform tasks like filling forms, navigating sites, and injecting userscripts. [3,4] When the agent encounters ambiguity, CAPTCHAs, or risky actions, it pauses and requests human input, allowing for a continuous co-driving experience where the human can intervene or adapt the agent's actions. [3,4]
- **Primary Intended Users:** The primary intended users include individuals who use AI models (Claude, GPT, Gemini, local Ollama, LM Studio, custom scripts) and want them to operate within their real browser sessions, teams and developers seeking a serious browser surface behind MCP without custom wrappers, users prioritizing local-first control, authenticated sessions, and strong security, and companies testing secure browser-based AI workflows and human-in-the-loop systems. [1,4] It is also for desktop users, server admins deploying autonomous agents remotely, and developers building custom clients or programmatically triggering agent workflows for the Tandem Engine. [1,4]
- **Necessary knowledge/expertise:** Users connecting AI agents to Tandem Browser need to understand how to configure their AI agent to speak MCP (Model Context Protocol) or HTTP and paste connection instructions. [1,3,4] Developers require knowledge of building custom clients, connecting external tools via MCP, or programmatically triggering agent workflows. [1,3,4] For advanced use, understanding concepts like accessibility trees, DOM, network requests, and developer tools can be beneficial. [1,3,4]
- **How to Use:** To use Tandem Browser, first download and install the appropriate binary for macOS Apple Silicon (v1.0.0) or Windows 11 x64 (v1.11.0). [3,4] For Linux, it can be run from source. [3,4] After launching, open 'Settings' -> 'Connected Agents' and scroll to 'Connect your AI to Tandem'. [3,4] Choose 'On this machine' for local AI or 'On another machine' for remote AI over Tailscale, then click 'Generate connection instructions'. [3,4] Copy these instructions and paste them into your AI agent's configuration. [3,4] The agent will then read Tandem's bootstrap surface and connect automatically. [3,4] Users can then interact with the AI agent within their browser session for various tasks. [3,4]
- **Targeted patient population:** Persons requiring insulin for the management of diabetes mellitus. [7] Specifically, for Control-IQ+ technology: individuals 2 years of age and greater with type 1 diabetes mellitus, and individuals 18 years of age and greater with type 2 diabetes mellitus. [7] It should not be used in anyone under the age of 2 years old with type 1 diabetes or under the age of 18 years old with type 2 diabetes, or in patients who require less than a total daily insulin amount. [7]
- **Patient consent or disclosure required or suggested?:** The Tandem Mobi system, including Control-IQ+ technology, is an RX ONLY device, meaning it requires a prescription.
