# [Skin Vision](https://www.skinvision.com/en-us) — Clinical Model Card

## 1) Metadata
- **Model Name:** Skin Vision [1]
- **Developer:** SkinVision [1]
- **Regulatory Approval Status:** CE Marked [2,3]
- **Regulatory Approval Date:** Not disclosed
- **Version:** Not disclosed
- **Last Updated:** 2026-07-25
- **Availability:** SkinVision has partnerships with health insurers, cancer clinics, and research universities in the United Kingdom, Australia, Germany, the Netherlands, and New Zealand, and skin checks may be covered by a user's health insurance provider. The service is not yet available in the United States; U.S. launch is planned pending applicable regulatory clearance. [3,1]

## 2) Summary
- **Summary:** SkinVision is a health technology company focused on increasing access to skin health awareness and care. It supports individuals in becoming more aware of their skin over time, helping them take a more active role in their health journey. The service is currently not available in the United States, pending regulatory clearance. [1]

## 3) Intended Use
- **Medical Purpose:** To increase access to skin health awareness and care, and to support individuals in becoming more aware of their skin over time. [1]
- **Acceptable Input:** A smartphone photo of a skin spot or mole. [3]
- **Output:** A risk indication for the most common types of skin cancer, delivered within 30–60 seconds, with a recommendation on next steps, including whether to see a healthcare professional. [3]
- **Role in Decision-Making:** To help individuals take a more active role in their skin health journey by increasing awareness and accessibility to information. [1]
- **Intended Users:** Individuals seeking to increase their awareness and understanding of their skin health. [1]
- **Target Patient Population:** The target patient population includes individuals and people seeking to increase their skin health awareness and care, become more aware of their skin over time, and take a more active role in their health journey.

## 4) Risks and Limitations
- **Known Performance Limitations:** SkinVision is not yet available in the United States; U.S. availability is subject to FDA authorization and U.S. launch is planned pending applicable regulatory clearance.
- **Skin Type Representation & Equity:** Not disclosed
- **Required Clinician Oversight:** SkinVision is not intended to replace traditional methods for evaluating skin cancer risk, does not provide a diagnosis, and is not a substitute for visits to a healthcare professional. [3]

## 5) Mechanism and Development
- **Model Type:** AI-based algorithm for skin cancer risk assessment, combining smartphone image analysis with oversight from dermatologists. [2]
- **Training Data:** Not disclosed
- **Model Development & Validation:** The company collaborates with medical experts, researchers, and leading institutions, including Mayo Clinic, for ongoing research and development in digital health. [1]
- **Clinical Trial:** Not disclosed
- **Performance:** The SkinVision algorithm has a sensitivity greater than 90% in detecting the three most common types of skin cancer (malignant melanoma, squamous cell carcinoma, and basal cell carcinoma) and a specificity of 89%, measured on SkinVision's user population. Users have received more than 3.5 million risk assessments, and the service has identified over 50,000 cases of melanoma and other skin cancers. [2,3]
- **Limitations in Evaluating Performance:** Not disclosed

## 6) Governance and Monitoring
- **Plans for Ongoing Monitoring:** SkinVision invests in ongoing research and clinical evidence collection, supported by a quality system involving dermatologists worldwide, to maintain and improve the reliability and accuracy of its risk-assessment service. [2]
