## 2) Summary
- **Summary:** Forus is an AI-powered platform embedded into EHR workflows that automates prior authorizations, appeals, financial assistance, and pharmacy routing. It takes prescription and patient information as input to provide comprehensive support for medication access. The platform's role is to accelerate time to therapy and ensure patients receive their prescribed treatments. [2,5,6]

## 3) Intended Use
- **Medical Purpose:** To accelerate and simplify patient access to prescribed medications by automating administrative processes such as prior authorizations, appeals, financial assistance, and pharmacy routing across all drugs, insurance plans, and pharmacies nationwide. [2,5,6]
- **Acceptable Input:** The platform integrates with electronic health record (EHR) systems and physician workflows to accept prescription information and patient data. [1,2,5,6]
- **Output:** Automated insurance authorizations, generated appeal letters, identification of financial assistance programs, optimized pharmacy routing, coverage checks for insurance and out-of-pocket costs, cost-saving options like coupons, and real-time updates for patients and providers. [2,5,6]
- **Role in Decision-Making:** Forus assists clinicians by automating administrative tasks and providing clear, proactive information about the medication access path, thereby giving doctors confidence to prescribe effective treatments and manage the process more efficiently. [2,6,7]
- **Intended Users:** The intended users are clinicians (doctors, medical practices, health systems), pharmacies, payers, and biopharma companies. Clinicians use it to manage prescription access for their patients. [1,5,6]
- **Target Patient Population:** The platform supports millions of patients annually across various specialties, including those with digestive diseases and dermatology conditions such as acne, eczema, and psoriasis. [5,6]
