# Tandem — Clinical Model Card

## 2) Summary
- **Summary:** Tandem is an AI-powered platform that automates end-to-end medication access workflows (prior authorization submission, appeals, pharmacy routing, affordability/patient assistance enrollment, and patient communication).

## 3) Intended Use
- **Medical Purpose:** Automate steps of obtaining prior authorization for medications, including PA form generation/submission, appeals, pharmacy navigation, and patient communication.
- **Acceptable Input:** Inputs include structured and unstructured clinical/admin data; outputs include structured forms, letters, routing instructions, portal updates, and SMS messages.
- **Output:** Completed PA forms, payer follow-up coordination, appeal letters (clinician-approved), enrollment workflows, pharmacy routing, and real-time patient status communications via text; staff dashboards.
- **Role in Decision-Making:** Provides adjunctive referral support for physicians evaluating suspicious lesions; does not replace clinician judgment, biopsy, or definitive diagnosis.
- **Intended Users:** Physicians, nurse practitioners, and practice staff at outpatient practices; also used in workflows involving patients waiting to receive medications. No specialized technical knowledge described; requires provider e-prescribing capability.
- **Target Patient Population:** U.S. patients requiring medications with prior authorization.

## 4) Risks and Limitations
- **Known Performance Limitations:** Described limitations include scope focused on medication PAs (not all categories), variability in performance for complex cases or non-responsive patients, not a full replacement for PA specialists, and limited public disclosure of model error rates.
- **Skin Type Representation & Equity:** Not disclosed
- **Required Clinician Oversight:** A prescribing physician (not necessarily a dermatologist) must operate the device and interpret its output. Definitive diagnosis requires clinician judgment and biopsy or pathology confirmation where clinically indicated.

## 5) Mechanism and Development
- **Model Type:** Workflow automation / administrative support with human review. AI-powered workflow automation platform using a human-in-the-loop approach; combines NLP and machine learning with payer/workflow automation.
