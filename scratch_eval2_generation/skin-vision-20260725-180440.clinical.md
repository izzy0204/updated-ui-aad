## 2) Summary
- **Summary:** SkinVision is a health technology solution that enables users to upload images of skin lesions. It provides AI-assisted risk assessments to increase skin health awareness and support early detection of skin cancer, prompting users to seek medical advice if a high-risk assessment is given. [2]

## 3) Intended Use
- **Medical Purpose:** To increase skin health awareness and facilitate the early detection of skin cancer by providing risk assessments of skin lesions. [2]
- **Acceptable Input:** Images of skin lesions (JPEG/PNG/BMP/TIFF/WebP, max 20 MB) and patient metadata (age, sex, anatomical site). [2]
- **Output:** The model provides an AI-assisted classification of skin lesions, a confidence score, prediction entropy, and a Grad-CAM heatmap for visual explanation. This output is used to generate a risk assessment (e.g., high-risk) or classification (e.g., Melanoma, Nevus, Basal Cell Carcinoma). [2]
- **Role in Decision-Making:** The AI predictions are advisory only and are intended to assist users in becoming more aware of their skin health and to prompt them to consult a qualified dermatologist for professional medical diagnosis. The model does not replace clinical judgment. [2]
- **Intended Users:** Individuals who want to monitor their skin over time and increase their awareness of skin health. [2]
- **Target Patient Population:** Not explicitly specified in the provided sources. [2]
