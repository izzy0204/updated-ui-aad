# [DermGPT](https://www.dermgpt.com/) — Clinical Model Card

## 1) Metadata
- **Model Name:** DermGPT [2]
- **Developer:** Dr. Faranak "Fara" Kamangar MD [2]
- **Regulatory Approval Status:** Regulatory Status Unknown
- **Regulatory Approval Date:** Not disclosed
- **Version:** DermGPT was recently unveiled in 2023, with discussions of the 'new innovation' occurring as early as August 25, 2023, and in a November 2023 publication. The platform is continuously updated and improved; however, no specific version number is provided in the context.
- **Last Updated:** 2026-07-25
- **Availability:** Available for dermatologists nationwide, free to use by signing up at www.dermgpt.com.

## 2) Summary
- **Summary:** DermGPT is an AI chatbot that accepts natural language questions from dermatologists and staff. It generates dermatology-specific answers, summaries, and documents to streamline clinical tasks like prior authorizations and patient education. The tool serves as an assistant to improve clinic productivity and access to information, without replacing medical care or decision-making. [2]

## 3) Intended Use
- **Medical Purpose:** To assist dermatologists and their staff by streamlining clinical tasks, improving access to dermatologic data, and enhancing practice efficiency through AI-powered information retrieval, summarization, and document generation. [2]
- **Acceptable Input:** Natural language questions and queries from dermatologists and their staff. [2]
- **Output:** The model generates dermatology-specific answers, summaries of diagnoses and treatment guidelines, suggestions for labs/imaging/procedures, automated documentation (e.g., visit notes, orders, prescriptions), insurance authorization letters, patient educational handouts, and study guides for board exams. This output is based on its proprietary database of over 3,000 peer-reviewed articles and trusted, physician-approved sources. [2]
- **Role in Decision-Making:** An assistant tool to save time and improve access to dermatologic data; it does not replace medical care or decision-making and should not be used as a stand-alone decision maker. It is most powerful when paired with a dermatology provider or staff. [2]
- **Intended Users:** Board-certified dermatologists, dermatology residents, RNs, MAs, and ancillary staff who possess the clinical knowledge to complement the AI's data-driven tasks with human creativity and problem-solving. [2]
- **Target Patient Population:** Not directly applicable as it's a tool for clinicians, but aims to improve patient care and access to cutting-edge medicines for patients with various dermatological conditions, including inflammatory skin diseases like psoriasis and eczema. [2]

## 4) Risks and Limitations
- **Known Performance Limitations:** DermGPT is an AI tool and, like all AI tools, lacks human creativity, intuition, and the ability to think outside the box. It is most powerful when paired with a dermatology provider or staff who can complement its skills with higher-level creative problem-solving. [2]
- **Skin Type Representation & Equity:** The DermGPT AI platform is a language-based model, not a visual AI tool, and is built on over 3,000 peer-reviewed articles and trusted, physician-approved sources. While the context does not specify how DermGPT's internal data or algorithms ensure equitable skin type representation, its founder, Dr. Faranak Kamangar, has hosted podcast episodes discussing relevant topics such as "biased behavior in healthcare" related to AI and "melanoma in skin of color." This demonstrates an awareness and engagement with issues of equity and diverse skin conditions within the broader dermatology and AI landscape.
- **Required Clinician Oversight:** Yes, DermGPT requires oversight from a dermatology provider or staff to complement its skills with higher-level creative problem-solving and ensure appropriate medical care and decision-making. [2]

## 5) Mechanism and Development
- **Model Type:** Language-based AI chatbot [2]
- **Training Data:** Built on more than 3,000 peer-reviewed dermatology articles, forming a proprietary, expert-curated, dermatology-specific database. It is continuously updated.
- **Model Development & Validation:** Developed by Dr. Faranak Kamangar, a board-certified dermatologist with an engineering background, to be unique in the world of large language models due to its proprietary database of trusted, physician-approved sources.
- **Clinical Trial:** Not disclosed
- **Performance:** The model aims for specificity and reliability by drawing from its curated dermatology database, reducing errors and hallucinations common in generic large language models.
- **Limitations in Evaluating Performance:** DermGPT, like all AI tools, is not a human and lacks human creativity, intuition, and the ability to think outside the box. It should not be used as a stand-alone decision-maker and does not replace medical care or decision-making. Its most powerful use is when paired with a dermatology provider or staff who can complement its skills with their higher-level creative problem-solving.
