# [DermGPT](https://www.dermgpt.com/) — Clinical Model Card

## 1) Metadata
- **Model Name:** DermGPT [1,4,5]
- **Developer:** Dr. Faranak "Fara" Kamangar MD [1,4,5]
- **Regulatory Approval Status:** Regulatory Status Unknown
- **Regulatory Approval Date:** Not disclosed
- **Version:** DermGPT was recently unveiled at a limited press briefing (as of November 2023) and is continuously updated and improved.
- **Last Updated:** 2026-07-25
- **Availability:** DermGPT is an AI platform now used by thousands of dermatologists nationwide. It is free for dermatologists to use, who can sign up and start using it clinically at no cost.

## 2) Summary
- **Summary:** DermGPT is an AI chatbot that accepts natural language questions from dermatologists and staff. It generates dermatology-specific text, such as answers to clinical queries, patient education handouts, and prior authorization letters. Its role is to assist with clinical tasks, improve efficiency, and save time for healthcare providers. [2,5,6,7]

## 3) Intended Use
- **Medical Purpose:** DermGPT is an AI chatbot designed to streamline dermatology workflows, improve clinical efficiency, and enhance access to dermatologic data for healthcare providers. It assists with tasks such as generating prior authorizations, denial letters, patient education handouts, summarizing guidelines, and providing quick references for medication options. [2,5]
- **Acceptable Input:** Natural language questions or prompts related to dermatology. [2,5]
- **Output:** The model generates specific and reliable dermatology-related verbiage, including answers to clinical queries, prior authorization letters, patient education handouts, summaries of medical notes, and study guides, drawing from its curated dermatology-specific database. [2,5]
- **Role in Decision-Making:** DermGPT functions as an assistant to dermatologists and their staff, aiding in efficient and data-driven tasks. It is not intended to replace medical care or decision-making and should not be used as a stand-alone decision maker. [2]
- **Intended Users:** Dermatologists and their staff, including RNs, MAs, and ancillary staff, who possess the clinical knowledge and problem-solving skills to complement the AI tool's capabilities. [2,4,5]
- **Target Patient Population:** The tool supports the care of patients across the spectrum of dermatological conditions by assisting their healthcare providers. [2]

## 4) Risks and Limitations
- **Known Performance Limitations:** The model lacks human creativity, intuition, and the ability to think outside the box, excelling primarily in efficient and data-driven tasks. It is not a human and should not be used as a stand-alone decision-maker. [2]
- **Skin Type Representation & Equity:** Not disclosed
- **Required Clinician Oversight:** DermGPT requires clinician oversight; it is most powerful when paired with a dermatology provider or staff who can complement its skills with higher-level creative problem-solving. [2]

## 5) Mechanism and Development
- **Model Type:** Language-based AI chatbot. [2,5]
- **Training Data:** The AI chatbot is built on a proprietary database of more than 3,000 peer-reviewed articles and trusted, physician-approved sources, making it more specific and reliable than generic large language models. [2,5]
- **Model Development & Validation:** DermGPT was developed by Faranak "Fara" Kamangar, MD, a board-certified dermatologist with an engineering background. The platform's proprietary database is continuously updated and improved, unlike some general AI models. [2,5]
- **Clinical Trial:** Not disclosed
- **Performance:** DermGPT is an AI-powered chatbot that improves clinic productivity and aims to streamline clinical tasks, improve access to dermatologic data, and save time for physicians. It allows dermatologists to ask questions in natural language and receive relevant, helpful answers, functioning as a more specific and reliable tool compared to generic language models. It is built on more than 3,000 peer-reviewed articles and a proprietary database, which helps it reduce errors like 'hallucinations'. DermGPT assists with common tasks such as writing insurance authorization letters, creating patient handouts, summarizing medical notes, generating prior authorizations and denial letters, and looking up dermatology information including diagnoses, treatments, and prior authorization criteria. Its use is intended to yield more efficient patient care and decreased wait times. DermGPT is currently used by thousands of dermatologists nationwide and is continuously updated and improved.
- **Limitations in Evaluating Performance:** DermGPT is not a human and lacks human creativity, intuition, and the ability to think outside the box. It should not be used as a stand-alone decision maker and does not replace medical care or decision-making, but functions as an assistant best paired with a dermatology provider or staff. [3]
