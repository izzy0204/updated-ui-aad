# [DermGPT](https://www.dermgpt.com/) — Clinical Model Card

## 1) Metadata
- **Model Name:** DermGPT [1]
- **Developer:** Dr. Faranak "Fara" Kamangar MD, board-certified dermatologist, Department Chair, Palo Alto Medical Foundation. [1]
- **Regulatory Approval Status:** Not Authorized / Consumer Tool
- **Regulatory Approval Date:** Not disclosed
- **Version:** Not versioned; a learned language model based on dermatology knowledge from more than 3,000 peer-reviewed articles and texts, not a versioned software release. [2]
- **Last Updated:** 2026-07-25
- **Availability:** Available for anyone to use online.

## 2) Summary
- **Summary:** DermGPT is a dermatology-focused AI chatbot, developed by a board-certified dermatologist, that allows dermatologists and staff to ask natural-language questions and receive cited answers drawn from more than 3,000 peer-reviewed dermatology articles and texts. It is intended as a practice-management and knowledge-access assistant rather than a stand-alone clinical decision maker. [1][2][7]

## 3) Intended Use
- **Medical Purpose:** Simplify everyday administrative tasks associated with dermatology practice management and improve access to dermatology knowledge, as an assistant rather than a stand-alone decision maker; does not replace medical care or clinical decision-making. [1]
- **Acceptable Input:** Natural-language prompts entered into the web chat app, e.g. questions about diagnoses, treatments, prior authorization letters, or differential diagnosis for a described presentation. [2]
- **Output:** Natural-language text answers with citations, drawn from a learned language model trained on more than 3,000 peer-reviewed dermatology articles and texts; outputs include text-based answers, patient handouts, and authorization letters. [1]
- **Role in Decision-Making:** A complementary tool rather than a sole decision-maker; the full underlying dataset is not fully disclosed, so healthcare professionals should continue independent clinical judgment to mitigate potential bias. [7]
- **Intended Users:** Dermatologists and dermatology practice staff (RNs, MAs, ancillary staff), as well as medical students, residents, and researchers. No specialized technical knowledge is required; preferred use is by medical professionals, though the developer notes users should proofread all AI-generated content for accuracy. [1]
- **Target Patient Population:** Not applicable to patients directly; intended for use by medical professionals rather than for a specific patient population.

## 4) Risks and Limitations
- **Known Performance Limitations:** Not a substitute for professional medical advice; limited to its database of peer-reviewed publications; like all AI tools, lacks human creativity and intuition and is most effective when paired with a dermatology provider or staff who complement it with higher-level clinical judgment. [1]
- **Skin Type Representation & Equity:** Not specified; the underlying peer-reviewed literature dataset's representativeness across skin tones/ethnicities has not been disclosed, so it is unclear whether the training corpus is inclusive of people of color.
- **Required Clinician Oversight:** No formal clinician oversight requirement, as this is an informational AI chatbot rather than a diagnostic device; standard risks apply, including potential for incorrect medical advice and bias if underlying literature is not inclusive. Users are advised to proofread all AI-generated content. [6]

## 5) Mechanism and Development
- **Model Type:** Informative large language model (LLM) trained on dermatology literature.
- **Training Data:** Trained on dermatology knowledge from more than 3,000 peer-reviewed articles and texts, including medical literature, clinical trials/studies, drug formulary information, expert consensus/reviews, and educational resources; full dataset composition is not fully disclosed.
- **Model Development & Validation:** Developed using peer-reviewed dermatology literature and other published sources; no independent validation study identified.
- **Clinical Trial:** Not disclosed
- **Performance:** Developer-reported: in the developer's own practice, DermGPT saved approximately 30-60 minutes per day previously spent on EHR documentation, allowing clinic days to finish earlier; no independent, peer-reviewed performance studies identified. [2]
- **Limitations in Evaluating Performance:** The full training dataset is not fully disclosed, so it is unknown whether the peer-reviewed literature used is representative of the entire patient population; no independent validation has been published; reported time-savings are anecdotal/developer-reported. [7]

## 6) Governance and Monitoring
- **Plans for Ongoing Monitoring:** Continuously updated and improved by the developer, intended to remain current with new dermatology literature. [1]
- **Scheduled Updates:** Continuous updates per the developer; no fixed schedule disclosed.
- **Inquiries / Report an Issue:** Not disclosed
